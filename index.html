Bridgeless Landsman converter

A power factor correction converter, redesigned against international harmonic limits. In an electric car this kind of stage sits at the front of the on-board charger. Changing the topology and slowing the switching cut the losses, and by design it also means less electromagnetic interference, although I did not measure that physically.

  • 25 % to under 5 %total harmonic distortion
  • 0.80 to 0.98power factor
  • Fewer switches, slower switchinglower switching losses, less interference by design

350 kW fast charging, as fast as the battery safely allows

A simulation model of 350 kW fast charging, from the AC grid through the converters and a liquid-cooled cable into the battery. The BMS code charges as fast as the pack allows and then derates the current against temperature, state of charge and the cell manufacturer’s own limits, with thermal management keeping the pack in range. The goal is the shortest charge that stays inside safe limits and protects the life of the battery, with state-of-health logic in the code.

  • 350 kWfast charging simulated
  • 720 Apeak charging current, derated from there
  • Derating inputstemperature, state of charge, and the cell manufacturer’s limits
  • Thermal managementpack held in its safe range while charging
  • State of healthlogic in the BMS code to protect battery life
  • 97 % and 96 %AC/DC and DC/DC efficiency, validated
  • −20 °C to 50 °Ctemperature extremes modelled
  • MATLAB GUIParameters, Graphs and Sankey tabs
  • MATLAB
