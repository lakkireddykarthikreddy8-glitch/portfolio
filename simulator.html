An illustrative recreation of the GUI from my master’s thesis. It keeps the structure (ambient temperature, a run button, the chain from grid to battery, and Parameters, Graphs and Sankey tabs), but the physics is simplified and the battery, thermal and limit values are assumed placeholders. Only the 97 % AC/DC and 96 % DC/DC efficiencies come from my validated results. None of the curves below are thesis data.

Assumptions and options
  • AC/DC efficiency: 97 % (validated result)
  • DC/DC efficiency: 96 % (validated result)
  • DC link voltage: 520 V
  • Cable current limit: 500 A, derated from 60 °C
  • Battery current limit: 500 A (assumed)
  • Pack voltage: 330 V at 0 % to 420 V at 100 % (assumed)
  • Optimal pack range: 20 to 40 °C, current derated outside it (assumed)
  • Pack resistance: 20 mΩ at 25 °C, higher when cold (assumed)
  • Pack heat capacity: 350 kJ/K (assumed)
  • Cooling and heating: up to 6 kW cooling, 9 kW heating, aim 25 to 35 °C (assumed)
  • Cable coolant: 25 °C, or ambient plus 8 K if hotter (assumed)
  • Starting state: pack and cable at ambient temperature
